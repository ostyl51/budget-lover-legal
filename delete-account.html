Delete your Budget Lover account

App: Budget Lover, by Orestis Stylianidis · Last updated: 23 November 2026

You can delete your account and all your data yourself, in the app or by writing to us.

Want a copy first?

Deleting cannot be undone. If you want to keep your records, open Budget Lover and go to Settings > Legal & Data > Export my data. It gives you a file with everything we keep about you.

In the app

  1. Open Budget Lover and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings > Legal & Data > Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password (or with Google).

Your account is deleted at once.

If you cannot open the app

Write to ostyl51@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will confirm that it is you and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profile and settings, your income and expense records, categories, savings goals, debts, trips, your conversations with the AI Advisor, the AI’s notes about you and your reports of AI answers, the data kept on your phone, and your sign-in. This cannot be undone.

What we may keep, and for how long

Records of purchases that the law requires us to keep (for as long as tax and accounting law requires). Google may keep technical logs for a limited period under its own terms. Payments are handled by Google Play, which keeps its own records.

Before you delete

If you have a Pro subscription, cancel it first in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions: deleting the account does not cancel it.

Contact: Orestis Stylianidis, ostyl51@gmail.com. See also the Privacy Policy.
