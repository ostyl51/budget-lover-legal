1. Who we are

Budget Lover is a budgeting app provided by Orestis Stylianidis, Kyriakou Matsi 23, Tseri 2480, Nicosia, Cyprus (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of your personal data: we decide why and how it is used. You can reach us at ostyl51@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal data the Budget Lover app collects, why, who receives it, how long we keep it and what rights you have. It applies to the app and to the pages of this website. Please read it together with our Terms of Use.

3. The data we collect, and why

We collect what the app needs to work. Most of it is what you type in. We do not connect to your bank: we only have what you enter yourself.

  • Account data. Your email address, your password (we never see it: our sign-in provider keeps it in a protected form), your name, and a user ID. If you sign in with Google: the name, email address and picture link Google gives us. Our sign-in provider also records your IP address, for example when you sign up or sign in, to prevent abuse. We use this to create and secure your account (contract; our legitimate interest in security).
  • Your agreement. When you create an account we save the date and the version of the Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy that you accepted and, if you sign up with an email address, that you said you are 18 or older. This is our proof that you agreed (our legitimate interest, and a legal obligation where it applies).
  • Profile and settings. The name you gave, a short bio, your currency and language, your monthly income and budget, interests, salary schedule, notification settings, a small profile photo (reduced to at most 256 pixels), your App PIN (kept only as a salted hash, so we cannot read the PIN), your XP and badges, whether you have Pro, and counters that limit the daily use of the AI. We use these to run the features (contract).
  • Optional personal details. Age, gender, occupation, family status and the answers to the optional savings questionnaire (for example housing costs, children, debts, savings). You can leave all of it empty. We use it to personalise your savings plan and the advisor’s answers (your consent, which you can withdraw by clearing the fields).
  • Your money records. Income and expenses (titles, amounts, categories, dates, notes), custom categories, savings goals, debts and payments and, with Pro, trips and trip expenses. This is the core of the app (contract).
  • AI features. See section 4.
  • Reports of AI answers. If you report an answer from an AI feature, we save the report in your account: why you reported it, where the answer was shown, the text of the answer and the date. We read reports to improve and filter the answers (our legitimate interest, and the rules of Google Play for apps with AI).
  • On your phone. The app keeps an offline copy of your account on your device, some settings (language, lock settings, which notices you have already seen, an exchange-rate cache), AI-written summaries, scheduled reminders and the data for the home-screen widget. Photos you take to scan a receipt are deleted after the scan.
  • Purchases. If and when you buy Pro, it is paid for through Google Play. Google handles your payment details; we only learn whether your subscription is active. We keep the records of purchases that the law requires.
  • Technical data. Our service providers create an installation ID for the app and receive basic technical information (app and Android version, country, language, time zone) so that the app can load its settings and prove that it is genuine. We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics tools or crash-reporting tools.
  • When you write to us. Your email address and the content of your message, to answer you.

Please do not enter information about your health, beliefs or other sensitive matters in notes or in the AI chat, and do not enter other people’s personal data.

4. How the AI features work

Some features use Google’s Gemini AI through Firebase AI Logic: the AI Advisor, the receipt scanner, the “what if” explanations of your budget, the trip planner and trip advice, the subscription review, the monthly wrap-up and the translation of the text the AI wrote about you. Most of them are part of Pro. The app tells you when you are talking to an AI and not to a person.

  • What is sent when you use a feature: your question and your last few messages; a summary of your finances (amounts, categories, dates, the category of your upcoming bills, the names of your savings goals, the names of your subscriptions when you ask about them or run the subscription review, the type and amounts of your debts, and your current trip); your age range (for example “25-34”) if you entered your age; the notes the AI saved about you; or, for the scanner, the photo of the receipt and the names of your categories.
  • What is not sent: your name, your email address, your occupation, gender or family status, the titles and notes of ordinary expenses, your PIN, or your location.
  • How Google treats it. We use the Gemini API through Firebase AI Logic under Google’s terms for developers. Google keeps prompts and responses for a limited period to detect and prevent abuse.
  • What we keep. Your messages and the answers are saved in your account so that you can see the conversation and the advisor can remember it. You can delete them with “Reset memory & chat” in the advisor, or by deleting your account.
  • Accuracy and your control. AI answers can be wrong. They are general information based on what you entered, not financial, tax, investment or legal advice. No decision with legal or similarly significant effect on you is made by the AI. You can report any answer with the flag next to it or under it; see section 3.

5. Who receives your data

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Remote Config, App Check, Google Cloud, the Gemini API, Google Sign-In and Google Play), as our service provider (“processor”) under Google’s data processing terms.
  • Exchange-rate services (ExchangeRate-API at open.er-api.com and Frankfurter at api.frankfurter.dev) when you use a trip in another currency: they receive the pair of currencies and your IP address, and nothing else.
  • Our website host (GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. in the United States) when you open these pages: it receives your IP address and technical data, under GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Authorities, courts and advisers when the law requires it, and a buyer if we sell the business (we would tell you first).

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it for advertising.

6. Where your data is processed

Your account is stored in Google data centres in the European Union (the Google Cloud multi-region “eur3”: Belgium and the Netherlands). Google and its sub-processors may process data, including AI requests, in other countries, including the United States. When data leaves the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom we rely on safeguards such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses and, where it applies, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, as set out in Google’s data processing terms.

7. Our legal bases

  • To give you the app and the features you ask for: contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • For security, abuse prevention, daily limits, fixing errors and keeping proof of your agreement: our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • For the camera, notifications, biometric unlock and the optional personal details: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To keep purchase and accounting records: legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).

8. How long we keep your data

  • Your account, records and AI chats: as long as you have an account. The AI chat stays until you reset it. When you delete your account they are deleted from our database at once. Our providers may keep logs and copies for a limited period under their own terms.
  • Reports of AI answers: until you delete your account.
  • Purchase and accounting records: for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Messages you send us: 12 months after our last contact.
  • Data on your phone stays until you delete the account or uninstall the app.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you have the right to: know what we hold about you and receive a copy; correct it; have it deleted; restrict its use; receive it in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format; object to processing based on our legitimate interests; and withdraw your consent at any time. You can also complain to a supervisory authority: the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus (dataprotection.gov.cy), or the authority in the country where you live or work.

Much of this you can do in the app: edit your profile and details, export your records as a PDF, download a copy of all your data as a file (Settings > Legal & Data > Export my data), reset the AI memory and chat, report AI answers, and delete your account. For anything else write to ostyl51@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm who you are. We answer within one month; if a request is complex we may need up to two more months and we will tell you why.

10. Deleting your account

In the app: Settings > Legal & Data > Delete account. You confirm with your password (or with Google). We then delete your profile, records, goals, debts, trips, categories, AI chats and reports from our database, remove the data kept on your phone and delete your sign-in. This cannot be undone. If you cannot open the app, see how to delete your account or write to ostyl51@gmail.com from the email address of your account.

11. Children

Budget Lover is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

12. Security

Your data is held on Google’s infrastructure, which encrypts it in transit and at rest. Access rules allow each account to read and change only its own data. You can lock the app on your phone with a PIN (kept only as a salted hash) and with your fingerprint or face. No system is completely secure. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will tell you and the authority as the law requires.

13. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera, through the camera app of your phone, to scan a receipt or take a profile photo. The app itself has no camera permission.
  • Notifications, for bill reminders and alerts (with vibration, and re-scheduled after the phone restarts). You can switch them off in the app or in Android.
  • Fingerprint or face, handled by Android. The app only learns whether the check passed.
  • Internet and network state, to sync your account and use the AI.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone or storage.

14. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the app or the law changes. If the change is important we will tell you in the app before it applies. The date at the top shows the latest version.
