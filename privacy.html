1. Who we are

Budget Lover is a budgeting app provided by Orestis Stylianidis, Kyriakou Matsi 23, Tseri 2480, Nicosia, Cyprus (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of your personal data: we decide why and how it is used. You can reach us at ostyl51@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal data the Budget Lover app collects, why, who receives it, how long we keep it and what rights you have. It applies to the app and to the pages of this website. Please read it together with our Terms of Use.

3. The data we collect, and why

We collect what the app needs to work. Most of it is what you type in. We do not connect to your bank: we only have what you enter yourself.

  • Account data. Your email address, your password (we never see it: our sign-in provider keeps it in a protected form), your name, and a user ID. If you sign in with Google: the name, email address and picture link Google gives us. Our sign-in provider also records your IP address, for example when you sign up or sign in, to prevent abuse. We use this to create and secure your account (contract; our legitimate interest in security).
  • Your agreement. When you create an account we save the date and the version of the Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy that you accepted and that you confirmed you are 18 or older. You do this with a tick box when you sign up with an email address, or on a screen that appears right after your first sign-in with Google. This is our proof that you agreed (our legitimate interest, and a legal obligation where it applies).
  • Profile and settings. The name you gave, a short bio, your currency and language, your monthly income and budget, interests, salary schedule, notification settings, a small profile photo (reduced to at most 256 pixels), your App PIN (kept only as a salted hash, so we cannot read the PIN) and your XP and badges. We use these to run the features (contract).
  • Optional personal details. Your age and the answers to the optional savings questionnaire (for example marital status, housing costs, children, debts, savings). You can leave all of it empty. We use it to calculate your savings plan (your consent, which you can withdraw by clearing the fields).
  • Your money records. Income and expenses (titles, amounts, categories, dates, notes), custom categories, savings goals, debts and payments, and trips and trip expenses. This is the core of the app (contract).
  • On your phone. The app keeps an offline copy of your account on your device, some settings (language, lock settings, which notices you have already seen, an exchange-rate cache), scheduled reminders and the data for the home-screen widget.
  • Technical data. Google’s services create technical identifiers and receive basic technical information (such as your IP address, the app and Android version, country, language and time zone) whenever the app connects to them. We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics tools or crash-reporting tools.
  • When you write to us. Your email address and the content of your message, to answer you.

Please do not enter information about your health, beliefs or other sensitive matters in notes, and do not enter other people’s personal data.

4. Artificial intelligence

Budget Lover has no AI features at the moment. Nothing you enter is sent to any AI service. An AI Advisor is being developed and is not available yet. If we add AI features, we will update this policy first, tell you in the app what they send and to whom, and ask for your agreement where the law requires it.

5. Who receives your data

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Cloud and Google Sign-In), as our service provider (“processor”) under Google’s data processing terms.
  • Exchange-rate services (ExchangeRate-API at open.er-api.com and Frankfurter at api.frankfurter.dev) when you use a trip in another currency: they receive the pair of currencies and your IP address, and nothing else. The app tells you this before the first rate is fetched, and you can type the rate yourself instead.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com) when you make a PDF, either a statement of your records or the monthly wrap-up: the app downloads the fonts for the PDF from Google. Google receives your IP address and the names of the fonts, and nothing from your account.
  • Our website host (GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. in the United States) when you open these pages: it receives your IP address and technical data, under GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Authorities, courts and advisers when the law requires it, and a buyer if we sell the business (we would tell you first).

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it for advertising.

6. Where your data is processed

Your account is stored in Google data centres in the European Union (the Google Cloud multi-region “eur3”: Belgium and the Netherlands). Google and its sub-processors may process data in other countries, including the United States. When data leaves the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom we rely on safeguards such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses and, where it applies, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, as set out in Google’s data processing terms.

7. Our legal bases

  • To give you the app and the features you ask for: contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • For security, abuse prevention, daily limits, fixing errors and keeping proof of your agreement: our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • For the camera, notifications, biometric unlock and the optional personal details: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

8. How long we keep your data

  • Your account and records: as long as you have an account. When you delete your account they are deleted from our database at once. Our providers may keep logs and copies for a limited period under their own terms.
  • What an earlier test version of the app saved for features that no longer exist (AI chats, reports of AI answers, a Pro setting): deleted from your account when you open the current version.
  • Messages you send us: 12 months after our last contact.
  • Data on your phone stays until you delete the account or uninstall the app.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you have the right to: know what we hold about you and receive a copy; correct it; have it deleted; restrict its use; receive it in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format; object to processing based on our legitimate interests; and withdraw your consent at any time. You can also complain to a supervisory authority: the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus (dataprotection.gov.cy), or the authority in the country where you live or work.

Much of this you can do in the app: edit your profile and details, export your records as a PDF, download a copy of all your data as a file (Settings > Legal & Data > Export my data), and delete your account. For anything else write to ostyl51@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm who you are. We answer within one month; if a request is complex we may need up to two more months and we will tell you why.

10. Deleting your account

In the app: Settings > Legal & Data > Delete account. You confirm with your password (or with Google). We then delete your profile, records, goals, debts, trips and categories from our database, remove the data kept on your phone and delete your sign-in. This cannot be undone. If you cannot open the app, see how to delete your account or write to ostyl51@gmail.com from the email address of your account.

11. Children

Budget Lover is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

12. Security

Your data is held on Google’s infrastructure, which encrypts it in transit and at rest. Access rules allow each account to read and change only its own data. You can lock the app on your phone with a PIN (kept only as a salted hash) and with your fingerprint or face. No system is completely secure. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will tell you and the authority as the law requires.

13. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera, through the camera app of your phone, to take a profile photo. The app itself has no camera permission.
  • Notifications, for bill reminders and alerts (with vibration, and re-scheduled after the phone restarts). You can switch them off in the app or in Android.
  • Fingerprint or face, handled by Android. The app only learns whether the check passed.
  • Internet and network state, to sync your account and to get exchange rates.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone or storage.

14. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the app or the law changes. If the change is important we will tell you in the app before it applies. The date at the top shows the latest version.
